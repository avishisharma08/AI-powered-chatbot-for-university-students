1. Attendance Policy
1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement

All students are expected to attend lectures, practical sessions, tutorials, and other academic activities as scheduled by the institution. Students must maintain the minimum attendance percentage prescribed by the university and college.

Sample rule for RAG testing: Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear in semester examinations, subject to applicable university regulations.

1.2 Attendance Calculation

Attendance is calculated based on the number of classes attended divided by the total number of classes conducted, multiplied by 100.

Attendance Percentage = (Number of Classes Attended / Total Classes Conducted) × 100

For example, if a student attends 36 out of 45 classes, their attendance is 80%.

1.3 Shortage of Attendance

Students with attendance below the required percentage may be detained from appearing in the semester examination, according to applicable university rules. Students should regularly check their attendance records and report any discrepancies to the concerned faculty member or department.

1.4 Medical Leave

Students who are absent due to illness or medical emergencies should inform their class coordinator and submit supporting medical documents as required. Submission of medical documents does not automatically guarantee that attendance will be granted or that a shortage will be condoned.

1.5 Attendance Monitoring

Students can check their attendance through the institution's designated student portal or by contacting their class coordinator. Any discrepancy should be reported promptly.
