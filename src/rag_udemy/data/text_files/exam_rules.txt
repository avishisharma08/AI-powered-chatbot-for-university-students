7. Examination Rules and Regulations
7.1 Examination Eligibility

Students must fulfil the applicable attendance, registration, fee, and academic requirements to be eligible for semester examinations. Eligibility is determined according to the current university regulations and institutional notices.

7.2 Examination Schedule

The examination timetable is published through official college or university communication channels. Students are responsible for checking the date, time, venue, and instructions for each examination.

7.3 Admit Card

Students must carry a valid examination admit card or hall ticket and their college identity card, as required by the examination authority. Entry may be denied if mandatory identification documents are missing, subject to the applicable examination rules.

7.4 Reporting Time

Students should arrive at the examination venue before the reporting time mentioned in the official instructions. Late entry, if permitted, is governed by the rules issued by the examination authority.

7.5 Permitted Materials

Students may bring only the stationery and other materials expressly permitted for the examination. Calculators, formula sheets, electronic devices, and other reference materials are allowed only when specifically authorised.

7.6 Prohibited Activities

The following are prohibited during examinations:

Copying from another student or attempting to communicate answers.
Carrying unauthorised notes, books, or electronic devices.
Using a mobile phone or smartwatch without explicit permission.
Impersonating another candidate or allowing impersonation.
Exchanging answer sheets or unauthorised materials.
Disrupting the examination or disobeying the invigilator's instructions.
7.7 Examination Hall Conduct

Students must maintain silence and follow all instructions issued by the invigilator. Students must not leave their seats or the examination hall without permission.

Answer sheets and other examination materials must be submitted as instructed. Students must not remove restricted examination materials from the hall.

7.8 Unfair Means

Any suspected use of unfair means will be handled according to the applicable university and institutional examination regulations. The consequences may include cancellation of the examination, withholding of results, or other penalties where prescribed.

7.9 Absence from Examination

Students who are unable to attend an examination due to illness or an emergency should inform the appropriate college authority and follow the university's prescribed procedure. Medical or other supporting documents may be required. Permission for a re-examination or special consideration is not automatic.

7.10 Results and Revaluation

Examination results are published through the official university or college portal. Students seeking scrutiny, revaluation, or other result-related remedies must apply within the notified deadline and follow the prescribed process.

7.11 Examination Queries

Students should contact the examination cell or the designated department representative for clarification about examination dates, admit cards, eligibility, results, and revaluation procedures.
