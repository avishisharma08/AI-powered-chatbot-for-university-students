. College Fees and Payment Rules
4.1 Fee Payment

All students must pay tuition fees and other applicable institutional charges according to the fee schedule issued by the college. Students should use the approved payment methods and retain transaction receipts for future reference.

4.2 Types of Fees

Depending on the institution's fee structure, students may be required to pay:

Tuition fees
Registration or admission fees
Examination fees
Library fees
Laboratory fees
Hostel and mess fees, where applicable
Security deposits
Other notified institutional charges
4.3 Payment Deadlines

Students must pay all applicable fees before the notified deadline. The college may issue reminders or notices regarding outstanding payments.

Late fees, if applicable, will be charged according to the official fee circular. Students should contact the accounts office if they encounter payment difficulties or need clarification regarding outstanding dues.

4.4 Fee Receipts

Students should download or collect an official receipt after every payment. The receipt should be retained until the payment is reflected in the institution's records.

4.5 Refunds

Refunds, including admission withdrawals and refundable security deposits, are governed by the institution's published refund policy and applicable university or regulatory rules. Students must submit the prescribed refund request with supporting documents.

4.6 Outstanding Dues

Students with unpaid fees may be subject to restrictions on services or administrative processes where permitted by institutional regulations. Any restriction relating to examination eligibility must comply with the applicable university and regulatory rules.

4.7 Fee-Related Queries

For questions regarding fee amounts, due dates, payment failures, receipts, or refunds, students should contact the accounts department through the official college communication channels.
