2. Library Rules and Regulations
2.1 Library Membership

All enrolled students are eligible to use the college library after completing the required registration process. Students must carry their valid college ID card to access library services and borrow books.

2.2 Library Timings

The library operates during the working hours notified by the institution. Timings may vary during examinations, holidays, and special academic events. Students should check the latest library notice for updated opening and closing hours.

2.3 Book Borrowing Rules
Students must present their valid college ID card when borrowing books.
Books may be issued only to registered library members.
The number of books permitted and the loan period are determined by the library's current borrowing policy.
Reference books, journals, newspapers, and other restricted materials may only be used within the library.
Borrowed books must be returned or renewed before their due date, subject to library rules.
2.4 Late Return and Fines

Students are responsible for returning books on or before the due date. Late returns may attract a fine according to the library's prescribed fine schedule.

 A late fine of ₹5 per book per day may be charged for overdue books. The actual fine and any maximum limit must be confirmed from the official library policy.

2.5 Lost or Damaged Books

Students who lose or damage library books must report the incident to the librarian. They may be required to replace the book or pay the prescribed replacement cost and any applicable charges.

2.6 Library Conduct
Maintain silence and avoid disturbing other readers.
Keep mobile phones on silent mode.
Do not write, underline, fold, or tear pages of library materials.
Do not consume food or drinks in designated reading areas.
Return books to the appropriate desk or shelf as instructed.
Follow the librarian's instructions and all posted notices.
