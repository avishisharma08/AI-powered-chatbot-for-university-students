College Uniform and Dress Code
5.1 Uniform Requirement

Students must wear the prescribed college uniform on all designated working days and during official academic activities when the uniform is required.

The exact uniform design, colours, footwear, and accessories are determined by the institution's official dress-code notice.

5.2 General Dress Code

Students must dress neatly and appropriately for academic and institutional activities. Clothing should comply with the college's published dress-code requirements.

5.3 Uniform During Practical Sessions

Students must follow any additional clothing and safety requirements applicable to laboratories, workshops, and other practical sessions. Protective equipment, such as lab coats or safety footwear, must be worn where required.

5.4 Identity Card

Students must carry and display their valid college identity card as directed by the institution. The ID card may be required for entry into academic buildings, examinations, the library, and other campus facilities.

5.5 Non-Compliance

Students who do not comply with the dress code may be asked to correct the issue or report to the designated faculty or administrative authority. Any further action must follow the institution's disciplinary procedures.

5.6 Special Circumstances

Students who require a dress-code exemption for medical, religious, accessibility, or other valid reasons should contact the appropriate college authority and follow the prescribed approval process.
